# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing customer shopping behavior using Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI. The project focuses on understanding customer spending patterns, product performance, subscription behavior, customer segments, and purchasing trends.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **3,900 customer transactions** across different product categories to identify patterns in customer behavior and generate business insights.

The analysis covers:

- Customer demographics and purchasing behavior
- Revenue and sales performance
- Subscription vs. non-subscription behavior
- Customer segmentation
- Product ratings and product performance
- Discount usage
- Shipping preferences
- Revenue contribution by age group

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python** – Data cleaning, EDA & feature engineering
- **Pandas** – Data manipulation and analysis
- **PostgreSQL** – Database storage and SQL analysis
- **SQL** – Business analysis and customer segmentation
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- **DAX** – Measures and analytical calculations

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation & Cleaning

The dataset contains **3,900 rows and 18 columns**, including customer demographics, purchase details, shopping behavior, ratings, subscription status, and shipping information.

Data preparation included:

- Loading the dataset using Pandas
- Exploring the dataset using `info()` and `describe()`
- Handling missing review ratings using category-level median imputation
- Standardizing column names using snake_case
- Creating an `age_group` feature
- Creating `purchase_frequency_days`
- Checking for redundant fields
- Removing the `promo_code_used` column
- Loading the cleaned dataset into PostgreSQL

## 🔎 SQL Business Analysis

PostgreSQL was used to answer business-focused questions, including:

- Revenue generated by gender
- High-spending customers using discounts
- Top-rated products
- Average purchase amount by shipping type
- Subscriber vs. non-subscriber spending and revenue
- Products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases
- Customer segmentation into New, Returning, and Loyal customers
- Top products within each category
- Relationship between repeat purchases and subscriptions
- Revenue contribution by age group

## 💡 Key Findings

Some of the findings from the analysis include:

- Young-adult customers generated the highest revenue among the analyzed age groups.
- Clothing generated the highest revenue among the product categories.
- Non-subscribers represented the larger customer group and generated higher total revenue in the analyzed dataset.
- Customers were segmented into **New, Returning, and Loyal** groups based on purchase history.
- Hat, Sneakers, Coat, Sweater, and Pants were among the products with the highest proportion of discounted purchases.
- Express shipping had a higher average purchase amount than Standard shipping.
- Gloves, Sandals, Boots, Hat, and Skirt were among the highest-rated products.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive benefits to encourage customers to subscribe.
- **Customer Loyalty:** Reward repeat buyers and encourage movement toward the Loyal segment.
- **Review Discount Strategy:** Balance discount-driven sales with margin control.
- **Product Positioning:** Highlight highly rated and frequently purchased products in marketing campaigns.
- **Targeted Marketing:** Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue customer groups and relevant shipping segments.
